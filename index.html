This map shows suicide deaths across Rhode Island municipalities for different time periods. Users can explore deaths by municipality of injury, municipality of residence, or the suicide rate per 100,000 population.

The color scale represents ranges of values to help visualize geographic patterns across towns.

Rates may be suppressed or marked with an asterisk (*) when counts are small to protect confidentiality and ensure statistical reliability, consistent with RIDOH small number reporting policies.

This visualization is intended to support public health awareness and should be interpreted alongside broader demographic and population context.
